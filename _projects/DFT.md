## What is DFT
Density Functional Theory is a quantum mechanical modeling method used to investigate the electronic structure of many-body systems, particularly atoms, molecules, and condensed phases.

## Project Goals
I will be working through some problems in textbooks, possibly online tutorials.

The end goal will be to use these DFT simulations to inform machine learning models in molecular dynamics.
