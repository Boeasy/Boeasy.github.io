Interactive 1-Qubit Polarization Visualization

|H⟩ = Horizontal, |V⟩ = Vertical

This visualization shows the relationship between quantum states and photon polarization. The left panel shows the quantum state on a unit circle, while the right panel displays the corresponding 3D photon wave.

How to Use:

  • Rotate the 3D plot: Click and drag to change viewing angle
  • Zoom: Use scroll wheel or pinch gestures
  • Pan: Hold shift and drag
  • Reset view: Double-click the plot

Key Features:

  • Green arrow: Current quantum state vector
  • Purple curve: 3D photon wave showing electric field
  • Yellow dot: Photon particle position
  • Dotted black lines: Electric field vectors
  • Dashed black line: Propagation direction
